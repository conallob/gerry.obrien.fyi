Beloved Father  ·  Scholar  ·  Pioneer

A Life Well Lived

Gerry O'Brien was a man of boundless curiosity and quiet warmth — a devoted father and an inspired educator who dedicated his life to exploring new frontiers in teaching and learning. He approached every room he entered with the same generous spirit: the unshakeable belief that the next question was always worth asking.

His family was his anchor. His students were his joy. And the world he leaves behind is richer for his having walked through it, eyes wide open, always looking for the wonder just beyond the visible.

Academic Achievement

✦

Master of Science

Augmented Reality in Education

Gerry was awarded his Master of Science for pioneering research into the application of Augmented Reality in educational settings — work that was years ahead of its time, and which now sits at the heart of how the world thinks about immersive, experiential learning. He didn't just study the future of education; he helped build it.

“AR doesn’t just augment reality — it augments understanding.”
